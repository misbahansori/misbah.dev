# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, clients, and other developers opening a personal site to decide whether Misbah is worth a conversation. They skim on a laptop or phone, often in a hurry.

## Product Purpose

A personal site for Misbah Ansori, a fullstack developer in Lampung. It should make the work history, stack, and shipped products readable in one sitting. Success is a visitor who can answer "what has he built, and for how long?" without hunting.

## Brand Personality

Instrument readout. Precise, dry, a little terminal. Voice of a plot on graph paper, not a pitch deck.

## Anti-references

Generic developer portfolios with gradient blobs, rounded card grids, and section kickers. Dribbble-soft SaaS. LinkedIn-resume clones. Editorial magazine layouts with display serif italics.

## Design Principles

- Show the data, don't decorate around it
- One visual language: dotted frames, mono labels, glyph charts
- Current work is the only thing that gets the accent
- If a number can be derived from the work history, derive it
- Keep the graph readable at a glance; details live next to it, not inside the ink

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast. Reduced motion already kills entrance animation. Decorative chart marks stay aria-hidden; titles, dates, and durations stay in text. Keyboard focus is a dashed accent outline.
